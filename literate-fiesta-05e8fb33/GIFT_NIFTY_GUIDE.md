# 📊 GIFT Nifty - Complete Trading Guide

## 🎯 What is GIFT Nifty?

**GIFT Nifty** = **Global Indices Financial Futures + Nifty 50**

- **USD-denominated** futures contract on Nifty 50 index
- Traded on **NSE International Exchange (NSE IX)** at GIFT City, Gujarat
- Provides **24-hour global market sentiment** on India's economy
- Replaced **SGX Nifty** futures as the global benchmark

---

| Feature | GIFT Nifty | Nifty 50 |
|---------|-----------|----------|
| **Location** | GIFT City, Gujarat (International) | NSE, India (Domestic) |
| **Currency** | USD | INR |
| **Trading Hours** | 6:30 AM - 2:45 AM (21 hours/day) | 9:15 AM - 3:30 PM (6 hours/day) |
| **Contract Size** | 25 units of Nifty | Variable |
| **Eligibility** | FPIs, NRIs, International investors | All Indian residents & institutions |
| **Regulator** | IFSCA (International) | SEBI (India) |
| **Settlement** | USD Cash-settled | INR Cash/Physical |
| **Best For** | Global sentiment, overnight moves | Direct market investment, intraday |

## 📊 Trading Signals from GIFT Nifty

### Pre-Market Opening Signal
The **gap between GIFT Nifty and previous Nifty 50 close** indicates:
- **Positive gap** (GIFT above close) → Market likely to open higher
- **Negative gap** (GIFT below close) → Market likely to open lower

### Example
```
Previous Nifty 50 Close: ₹23,414
GIFT Nifty at 8:00 AM:   ₹23,500 (+86 points)
Expected NSE Open:       Higher opening, likely bullish
```

### Overnight Momentum
- **GIFT moves during US/EU sessions** predict NSE opening
- **Check Session 2 (evening) moves** for next day's direction

## 🎯 Trading Strategies

### Strategy 1: Pre-Market Trading
```
Time: 6:30 AM - 7:00 AM IST
- Check GIFT Nifty gap
- If gap is positive with strong volume → Expect buy setup at NSE open
- If gap is negative → Expect sell setup
```

### Strategy 2: Overnight Hedging (for FPIs)
```
- Hold Indian stock positions during day
- Hedge with GIFT Nifty short at night if expecting US decline
- Exit hedge before NSE open
```

### Strategy 3: Global Sentiment Play
```
Time: Evening session (4:35 PM - 2:45 AM)
- Trade US/European market impact on GIFT
- Profit from overnight volatility
- Exit before Asia session if needed
```

## 💡 Key Trading Tips

✅ **DO**
- Use **stop-losses religiously** (futures are leveraged)
- Follow **position sizing** (1-2% risk per trade)
- Track **global market news** (US, Europe, China)
- Monitor **gap analysis** for NSE open
- Check **liquidity** before entering trades (GIFT has periods of low volume)

❌ **DON'T**
- Over-leverage positions
- Hold without stops
- Ignore global market movements
- Trade during low-volume sessions
- Risk capital you can't afford to lose

## 🔢 Margin & Costs

### Margin Requirements
- **Initial Margin**: ~10-15% of contract value
- **Maintenance Margin**: ~8-10%
- **Varies by volatility** (increases during market stress)

### Example (Approx)
```
Nifty Level: 23,400
Contract Value: 23,400 × 25 = ₹585,000
If margin is 12%: Margin needed = ~₹70,000 USD equivalent
```

- **Brokerage**: Varies by broker (~₹20-50 per contract or 0.01-0.02%)
- **Exchange Fees**: Included in brokerage
- **Taxes**: Treated as commodity futures (30% short-term capital gains)

## ⚠️ Risks & Warnings

🔴 **HIGH RISK FACTORS**
1. **Leverage**: 5-10x leverage amplifies both gains & losses

3. **Liquidity**: Less liquid than NSE Nifty futures
4. **Currency Risk**: USD settlement (INR depreciation/appreciation impacts)
5. **24-hour Trading**: No breaks means constant monitoring needed

## ❓ FAQs

**Q: Can I trade GIFT Nifty as an Indian retail trader?**  
A: Not directly without special eligibility. Use it for market direction signals instead.

**Q: What's the benefit of GIFT Nifty over NSE Nifty futures?**  
A: Nearly 24-hour global sentiment, pre-market signals, international participation.

**Q: How do I know if GIFT Nifty will help predict NSE opening?**  
A: The gap (GIFT close vs previous NSE close) often aligns with NSE open direction, though not always guaranteed.

**Q: What if I hold GIFT position till expiry?**  
A: You must roll it over to next month's contract 2-3 days before expiry.

**Q: Is GIFT Nifty more volatile than NSE Nifty?**  
A: Can be during low-liquidity periods (night sessions), but similar during overlaps.
